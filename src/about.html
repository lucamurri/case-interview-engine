About

Case Interview Engine is a free library of original consulting case interviews, built for candidates who want realistic practice without paywalls or sign-ups.

Who built it

I'm Luca Murri, a Master in Management candidate at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business (Class of 2027), recruiting for management consulting. Before Booth I worked in pharma marketing, and I'm especially interested in healthcare, energy and technology — which is why those industries feature heavily here.

I built this as the practice tool I wanted: cases that feel like real interviews, math you can trust, and a way to track progress without creating an account.

How the cases are made

  • Original. Every case is written from scratch. Clients are fictional, and none are adapted from published casebooks.
  • Illustrative numbers. Figures are realistic in shape but invented for practice — don't quote them as market data.
  • Checked math. Every calculation in every case is recomputed by an automated test before the site is published.

Privacy

There are no accounts, cookies or analytics. Your scores are saved only in your own browser.
