Fit interview practice

Most consulting interviews pair a case with a fit round. Pick a theme, answer out loud in about two minutes, then score yourself. Build a small bank of stories so you are never improvising.

How to answer

A simple five-part outline keeps answers tight. Total: about two minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Saying "we" for everything, so the interviewer cannot tell what you did.
  • Spending most of the time on background and rushing the actions and result.
  • Picking a story with no stakes or no conflict.
  • Giving a "failure" that is really a strength in disguise.
  • Skipping the result, or the lesson.
